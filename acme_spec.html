Dietary Polyphenols and Cardiovascular Outcomes

A Meta-Analysis of 34 Cohort Studies (2015–2024)

Journal of Nutritional Epidemiology, Vol. 18 — preprint

Abstract

We pooled 34 prospective cohort studies (n = 1.2M participants) examining the association between dietary polyphenol intake and incident cardiovascular events. High versus low intake was associated with a relative risk of 0.87 (95% CI 0.81–0.93) for composite cardiovascular endpoints, with the strongest associations observed for flavonoid subclasses. Heterogeneity was moderate (I² = 61%). Residual confounding by overall diet quality cannot be excluded.
